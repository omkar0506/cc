Core Innovation Architecture
  1. Resume Claim Extraction: Parse skills from PDF with verbatim evidence snippets.
  2. Role Requirement Matching: Map claims against required competencies.
  3. Confidence Self-Rating: Calibrate pre-interview confidence (1 to 5).
  4. Adaptive State Machine: Dynamic question difficulty & targeted follow-ups.
  5. Rubric Evaluation: Explainable criterion breakdown with deterministic fallback.
  6. 4 Evidence States: Fair classification without accusatory labels.
  7. Actionable Learning: Gap-driven learning checklist and attempt comparisons.

Explainable Evidence Classification

The engine never labels candidates dishonest. Every skill is categorized into one of four distinct transparent states:

Demonstrated

≥ 2 evaluated answers, average score ≥ 70%, including at least one medium or hard difficulty answer.

Partially demonstrated

Average ratio between 40% and 69%, or ≥ 70% with only a single easy foundational question.

Needs further assessment

Inconclusive evidence, such as ratio < 40% on one question or conflicting variance across answers.

Not yet assessed

Skill required by the job role but not yet encountered in completed interview questions.
